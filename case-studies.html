Who's Really Watching? Fixing Instagram's Story Replay Blind Spot

Read the full case study (PDF)

The problem

Instagram Pro shows creators a total story replay count, but never who rewatched or how often. A story replayed 340 times by 340 people looks identical to one replayed 34 times by 10 highly engaged viewers, even though those signal completely different levels of interest. On top of that, the viewer data disappears after 48 hours.

What I did

  • Surveyed 8 Instagram users to test the hypothesis and surface unprompted pain points
  • Used AI-assisted synthesis to group the responses into themes, then reviewed and corrected the output
  • Scored 4 candidate fixes with RICE and documented the reasoning behind each
  • Broke the chosen fix into 10 Jira stories across two sprints, with a timeline and risk plan

The interesting decision

A weekly digest email scored highest on RICE (1.40), but its confidence was only 50% because nobody had actually asked for it. The "Top Repeat Viewers" section scored 1.37, but was backed by two real complaints from the survey. I chose the lower-scoring option and wrote up why: I'd rather build something I know solves a real problem than chase a marginally better score built on a guess.

What the research changed

My original hypothesis was only raised unprompted by one of eight respondents. A different complaint came up stronger and unprompted: the viewer list vanishing after 48 hours. Rather than pick one, I scoped the fix to address both, since they're symptoms of the same underlying need. Half the respondents also said they rarely or never check story insights at all, which is a real limitation worth naming.

Full problem definition, RICE table, execution plan, and risk register are in the PDF.
